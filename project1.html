02 / Challenges → Solutions

Three problems, three deliberate answers.

Problem

Buyers can't tell fresh from old stock

Produce apps show a product photo, not when or where it was picked.

Solution

Harvest-time stamps on every listing

Each listing shows the garden, grower, and hours-since-harvest at a glance.

Problem

Small growers can't reach buyers

Listing produce usually means going through a distributor that takes the margin.

Solution

A seller flow simple enough for a first-timer

List a harvest in under [X] taps — no storefront setup required.

Problem

Delivery logistics don't exist yet

There's no fleet — Fruggo needs to work before delivery partners are onboarded.

Solution

Pickup-first, delivery as a phase-two toggle

Launch supports garden pickup; delivery partner flows are designed but gated behind a feature flag.

06 / User Journey

From craving to pickup.

Discover

Opens the app looking for something fresh nearby

Sees gardens sorted by distance and what's in season right now.

Evaluate

Compares listings by harvest time and grower

Checks the harvest-time stamp and grower profile before deciding.

Pain point — unsure how pickup timing works on first visit
Order

Places an order for garden pickup

Picks a pickup window and gets a confirmation with directions.

Pickup

Arrives, shows order code, collects produce

A quick, low-friction handoff — no app needed at the garden itself.

Return

Reorders from a favorite grower next season

Saved gardens surface again once their next harvest is listed.

16 / Reflection

What this project taught me.

Challenges

Designing a two-sided marketplace meant every screen had to work for two very different comfort levels with technology — buyers who shop daily, and growers who may open the app once a week.

Key Decisions

Launching pickup-only, with delivery designed but flagged off, kept the first version honest about what the business could actually fulfill.

Next Steps

Run the planned research and usability tests, then use real findings — not assumptions — to prioritize the delivery-partner rollout.
